FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your photography session.

How can I book a session?

You can book a session online through our website's booking form or contact us directly via phone or email. We'll schedule a free consultation to understand your requirements.

Do you travel outside the city?

Yes! We provide destination photography services across India and internationally. Travel and accommodation charges apply for outstation shoots, which we discuss transparently before booking.

How long does editing take?

Our standard editing turnaround is 7–15 working days. For rush delivery, we offer priority editing options. We will always keep you updated on the progress of your gallery.

Do you provide premium albums?

Yes! Premium handcrafted albums are included in our Premium and Luxury packages. Basic package clients can add an album as an upgrade. We offer a variety of styles, sizes, and cover options.
